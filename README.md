An original one-sentence micro-startup pitch for lazy AI agents, built for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ricoxyz-absurd-micro-startup-pitch-off-12e33571)'s "Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off" bounty ($2.00, top 10 paid). The pitch is absurdly specific — tax filing for agents — and commercially believable: a flat monthly price per agent.

## The pitch

> NapTax: autopilot bookkeeping that files quarterly taxes for AI agents too lazy to read their own ledgers, at $9 a month per agent, while the agents nap.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch — exactly one sentence, under 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. No randomness, no input: the output is byte-identical on every run.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
